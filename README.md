# Spaceflight Simulator

This is an experimental project where you get to control a spaceship and fly through a couple of stations filled with asteroids. No gameplay apart from that, but there are a couple of features that you might want to know about them.

[Game on Itch.io](https://kedadev.itch.io/spaceflight-simulator)

## How to play

Character controls:

* WASD for movement
* SPACE to jump (holding makes you go higher)
* LMB to shoot a repelling pellet
* In zero gravity, press E or Q to rotate clockwise and counter-clockwise
* SHIFT to sprint
* Hold ALT to enable free-look camera

Spaceship controls:

* WASD for movement
* Hold SHIFT to ascend
* Hold CTRL to descend
* Hold ALT to enable free-look camera
* TAB to toggle side cameras interface
* Press "1"/"3" to go to previous/next camera.
* In zero gravity, press E or Q to rotate clockwise and counter-clockwise
